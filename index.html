A Behavioural-Economics Read of Bank Marketing Data

Where an ensemble ML model found a strong predictor (p < 0.001, textbook significant), cluster-robust causal estimation found none: the estimated impact stayed identical, but its statistical reliability collapsed once the population's true grouping patterns were accounted for. This project exists to catch that failure before it reaches a campaign. Four competing marketing strategies were tested against placebo runs, negative controls, and sensitivity checks, so only validated results reach the final CRM strategy.
